Server Ruleset

Fair play policy enforced strictly via video evidence tickets

1. Mixpacking & Alliances
Herbivores and Carnivores are strictly forbidden from teaming up or traveling together. Carnivores of different species cannot hunt together.
2. Body Contesting & 3-Call Rule
When a body drops, carnivores contesting the kill must 3-Call (aggro call). If the claimer responds with a 3-call, combat initiates. If they 4-call (retreat call), they must surrender the kill and leave.
3. Nesting & Juvenile Protection
Spawn killing freshly hatched juveniles at a nest is prohibited. Parents have full legal rights to defend their nest radius against any approaching carnivore without warning.
